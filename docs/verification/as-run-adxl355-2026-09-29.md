# ADXL355 bench as-run record 2026-09-29

hardware: TC-17 to TC-21 with an EVAL-ADXL355Z on Pmod J1. Bringing the bench up found
three defects in the TE0715 board support and the bench itself, and one in
fprime-adxl355; the board support and bench were fixed before the assessed runs (see
[Defects found and fixed](#defects-found-and-fixed-during-bring-up)).

### TC-19 step 4

Frames take 120 µs at the bench's default SCLK of 1.116 MHz, of which the 15-byte
burst is about 107 µs. The slow frames are the stale ones and the frames right after
them. On a stale frame the manager emits `OperationFailed` and `LastStatus` telemetry
and the application `FrameIncomplete`; on the next frame the application emits
`FrameRecovered` and the manager `LastStatus` again. All of this runs on the
acquisition thread, including the formatting of the events' text; the console
printing itself runs on its own thread since the fixes below. The extra ~140 µs is
most likely this event work; it was not measured separately. With SCLK 8.93 MHz (run 2, `extra-4khz-at-8.93mhz.log`),
20000 of 20000 frames were complete in 31/32/51 µs; no frame was stale in that run, so
it did not exercise a fault edge.

The criterion is not met as specified. Two follow-ups: specify SCLK for TC-19 step 4
(flight uses about 10 MHz), and budget the cost of events on the acquisition path in
the deployment (text formatting, or text logging disabled). Neither is decided here.

## Defects found and fixed during bring-up

| # | Defect | Evidence (run 1) | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Zephyr v4.4.2's `cdns,spi` used the register address without mapping it; with the MMU on, the first write faulted at `0xe0007000` and Zephyr halted at boot | `uboot-load-1-mmu-fault.log` | Workspace patch `0001-spi-cdns-map-the-register-block`, a backport of upstream PR #115179 |
| 2 | With a GPIO chip select on MIO13, every transfer stalled with its bytes in the TX FIFO: the Zynq-7000 SPI master does not start while its SS0 input is low, and without SS0 on a pin that input is low | `diagnostic-*.log` (clock, reset, pin, GIC, GPIO registers correct; `diagnostic-ss0-input.log` test D stalls, E and F run) | SS0 on MIO13 is the chip select; the `cs-gpios` patch is no longer used by the bench |
| 3 | Bench: telemetry registered before `setup()` set the base IDs (nothing printed); events printed synchronously on the frame timing thread (up to 27 ms, missed ticks); console lines of different threads cut into each other (time slicing); `sensor_bench.py` read result lines before they were complete | `results.txt`, `transcript.log` of run 1 | `BaseIds` constants; active event and telemetry printers at the shell's priority, no time slicing, polled shell output, no output from the timing thread; the script waits for complete lines (new regression test) |

Between the first native-SS0 attempt, which read all ones, and runs 2 and 3, the
operator checked the set-up; the identity read then passed without reloading the
image. What was found: _operator to add_.
